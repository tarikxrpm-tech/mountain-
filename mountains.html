These mountain profiles are a starting point for learning about altitude, location, and the character of each landscape. High-altitude climbing requires professional preparation, permits, and experienced local guidance.

8,611 m

Karakoram · Pakistan / China

K2

A steep, remote giant near the Baltoro Glacier, known for the technical demands of its climbing routes.

8,849 m

Himalaya · Nepal / Tibet

Mount Everest

The highest point on Earth, framed by the Khumbu region's high trails, glaciers, and Sherpa communities.

8,126 m

Himalaya · Pakistan

Nanga Parbat

A dramatic Himalayan massif whose enormous Rupal Face rises above the surrounding valleys.

7,788 m

Karakoram · Pakistan

Rakaposhi

A vast snowy ridge rising above Hunza, admired from villages and trails across the valley.

8,051 m

Karakoram · Pakistan / China

Broad Peak

Pennine Alps · Switzerland / Italy

Matterhorn

Choose your pace

More than reaching
the summit.

Mountain experiences can be measured in miles, quiet moments, photographs, or time spent around a campfire. Start with what feels meaningful to you.

01 / CLIMB

Mountain climbing

Prepare for demanding terrain with skills training, acclimatization, the right permits, and qualified guides. No summit is worth ignoring safety.

02 / WALK

Hiking and trekking

Follow established routes through valleys and high passes. Choose a distance and altitude that fit your fitness and experience.

03 / STAY

Camping and photography

Camp responsibly where allowed, pack out everything you bring, and take time to notice the small details of the landscape.
