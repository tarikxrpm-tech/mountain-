Pakistan · High mountain wilderness

The Karakoram

Home to K2 and a remarkable concentration of high peaks, the Karakoram unfolds through glacier-carved valleys and small mountain communities. The classic trekking landscape around Baltoro and Concordia is remote, demanding, and extraordinary.

Many journeys begin in the Hunza or Skardu regions. Expect long travel days, high elevations, and changing weather; arrange licensed local support and allow time for acclimatization.

  • Known for: glaciers, high passes, and dramatic granite towers
  • Good starting point: Hunza Valley or Skardu, depending on route
  • Plan for: permits, local guides, altitude, and flexible schedules

Nepal & Tibet · The roof of the world

The Himalaya

Stretching across several countries, the Himalaya is a vast world of high valleys, sacred places, and routes shaped by local cultures. In Nepal, well-known trails pass through the Khumbu toward Everest viewpoints, while many quieter paths reward travelers who take time to explore beyond the busiest routes.

Respect local customs and protected areas, carry suitable layers, and choose a trek with realistic daily distances and rest days.

  • Known for: Everest, high-altitude trails, and living mountain cultures
  • Good starting point: Kathmandu and the relevant regional gateway
  • Plan for: seasonal access, permits, acclimatization, and local guidance

Switzerland · Trails for every pace

The Swiss Alps

From flower-filled valley paths to high mountain huts and glacier viewpoints, the Alps offer an inviting network of marked routes. Zermatt sits beneath the iconic Matterhorn, while railways and mountain lifts can make many viewpoints accessible without a technical climb.

Stay on marked trails, check lift and hut schedules in advance, and prepare for rapid changes in weather even on popular routes.

  • Known for: alpine villages, marked trails, and Matterhorn views
  • Good starting point: Zermatt, with train access from across Switzerland
  • Plan for: hut reservations, mountain weather, and trail conditions
